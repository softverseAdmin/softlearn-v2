# CHG-006

## Summary

Add gated lesson exams and a final course exam to student learning so lesson progression and course completion depend on passing seeded assessments with a 90% score.

## Reason

The accepted baseline already includes courses, lessons, progress tracking, and exams, but the current learning flow lets students finish lessons and courses without validating understanding at each stage. The student app now needs explicit lesson-level assessment gates, a final course exam, and a stricter completion rule so learning progress is measurable and auditable.

## Impacted units

- U4-5-lesson-viewing
- U4-7-lesson-progress-tracking
- U4-8-course-completion-tracking
- U5-4-exam-taking-ui
- U5-6-score-calculation
- U5-7-pass-fail-logic
- U5-9-exam-retake-policy

## Decision

Introduce a dedicated course-assessment flow for seeded course data that supports both single-select and multi-select questions in a modal UI. Each lesson exam unlocks only after the lesson is marked complete, the next lesson unlocks only after the lesson exam is passed with at least 90%, the final course exam unlocks only after the last lesson exam is passed, and a course is marked completed only after the final exam is passed with at least 90%. Retakes remain unlimited and no certificate is issued.
