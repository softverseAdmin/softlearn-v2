# CHG-007

## Summary

Add transcript-aware lesson delivery for courses: lessons may include a YouTube shared-link video URL plus a manually uploaded transcript, use only one uploaded thumbnail per course, and generate deterministic new exam questions from the transcript for each attempt while persisting only the resulting score.

## Reason

The course product direction now needs transcript-backed learning and fresh per-attempt assessments without relying on external AI services or direct YouTube transcript scraping.

## Impacted units

- Course and lesson content management
- Course preview and learning flow
- Course assessment generation and scoring
- Course media upload constraints

## Decision

Use manual transcript upload as the trusted transcript source, keep YouTube as URL-only video hosting, support a single course thumbnail upload, generate exam variants with deterministic rule-based logic from the stored transcript, and persist only assessment scores rather than per-attempt question/answer payloads.
