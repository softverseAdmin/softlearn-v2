# Technology selection rules

## Decision areas

- Backend
- Frontend
- Database
- Authentication
- Infrastructure
- AI and matching approach

## Core scoring criteria

| Criterion | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Speed of development | Helps deliver the platform incrementally with small units. |
| Scalability | Supports growth in learners, companies, exams, and matching traffic. |
| Maintainability | Keeps long-term unit-by-unit change safe. |
| Security | Protects student data, exam data, and admin capabilities. |
| AI feature support | Enables explainable matching and future recommendation enhancements. |
| Cost | Keeps early delivery practical. |
| Hiring/team skill availability | Reduces execution risk. |
| Deployment complexity | Keeps operations understandable from local to production. |

## Required comparison minimums

- At least 3 backend options
- At least 3 frontend options
- At least 2 database options
- At least 2 authentication options
- At least 2 hosting/deployment options

## Guardrails

- Do not declare a stack winner without documented comparison.
- Capture the selected combination and tradeoffs in ADR-001.
