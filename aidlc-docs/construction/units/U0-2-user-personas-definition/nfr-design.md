# NFR design: U0-2-user-personas-definition

## How NFRs are addressed

- Separate personas by role and responsibility.
- Call out privacy and access expectations where they matter.

## Tradeoffs

High-level personas are preferred over speculative demographic detail.

## Monitoring

Use review feedback to catch missing user groups before implementation starts.

## Testing approach

Check whether every major workflow has at least one supporting persona.
