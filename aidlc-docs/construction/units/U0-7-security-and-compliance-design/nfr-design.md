# NFR design: U0-7-security-and-compliance-design

## How NFRs are addressed

- Make privacy, consent, and audit requirements first-class.
- Keep the design provider-agnostic until the stack decision is accepted.

## Tradeoffs

Early security conservatism may slow some features, but it protects platform trust.

## Monitoring

Use review gates and future logging units to verify that controls stay visible.

## Testing approach

Review for coverage of access control, privacy visibility, and audit expectations.
