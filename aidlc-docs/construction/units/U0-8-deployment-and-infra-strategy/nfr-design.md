# NFR design: U0-8-deployment-and-infra-strategy

## How NFRs are addressed

- Separate simple-start from scalable-future guidance.
- Keep the strategy provider-neutral until the ADR is accepted.

## Tradeoffs

Avoiding early infra lock-in may delay detailed setup, but it keeps the decision reversible.

## Monitoring

Future infrastructure units should show how logging and monitoring evolve from this baseline.

## Testing approach

Review whether environments, CI/CD, backup, and observability are covered clearly.
