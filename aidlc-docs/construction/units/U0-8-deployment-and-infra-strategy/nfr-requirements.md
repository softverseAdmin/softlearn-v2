# NFR requirements: U0-8-deployment-and-infra-strategy

## Security

Future environments must support secret management, secure deployment, and audit-friendly operations.

## Privacy

Operational design must preserve student-data protection in all environments.

## Performance

The strategy should leave room for future scale without overbuilding the starting posture.

## Reliability

Backups, monitoring, and staged rollout expectations should be visible.

## Accessibility

Indirectly supports accessible delivery by keeping environments predictable.

## Maintainability

Operational guidance should be simple enough to evolve with the selected stack.
