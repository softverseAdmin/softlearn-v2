# NFR design: U0-6-ai-matching-strategy-design

## How NFRs are addressed

- Favor rule-based, weighted scoring first.
- Treat advanced AI as optional later enhancements.

## Tradeoffs

Explainability is prioritized over maximum recommendation sophistication in early phases.

## Monitoring

Future matching units should report how scores are calculated and exposed.

## Testing approach

Review scoring dimensions for clarity, auditability, and privacy alignment.
