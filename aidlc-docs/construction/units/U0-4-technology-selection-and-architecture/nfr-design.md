# NFR design: U0-4-technology-selection-and-architecture

## How NFRs are addressed

- Use a common scoring matrix across candidate options.
- Keep architecture conceptual until the decision is accepted.

## Tradeoffs

Delaying the decision protects design integrity but delays implementation kickoff.

## Monitoring

Track decision progress through `technology-decision.md` and ADR-001 reviews.

## Testing approach

Review whether every required category and criterion is covered before accepting the ADR.
