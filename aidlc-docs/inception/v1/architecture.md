# Inception v1 architecture

## Goal

Describe the target platform architecture conceptually without fixing a final technology stack.

## Conceptual layers

1. **Experience layer** for student, company, and admin interfaces
2. **Application layer** for identity, learning, exams, matching, vacancies, applications, interviews, and notifications
3. **Data layer** for transactional records, audit data, consent records, and reporting-oriented views
4. **Integration layer** for email, meeting links, storage, and future AI services

## Core domains

- Identity and access control
- Student profile and privacy
- Company profile and hiring workflows
- Learning and lesson progress
- Exams and scoring
- Matching and explainable recommendation
- Applications and interview scheduling
- Administration and audit

## Cross-cutting requirements

- Privacy-aware data access
- Auditable company access to student information
- Explainable matching decisions
- Clear separation between baseline design and later implementation stack
