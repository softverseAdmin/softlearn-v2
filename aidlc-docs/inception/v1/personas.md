# Inception v1 personas

## Foreign IT engineer learner

- Wants to improve technical readiness and find opportunities in Japan.
- Needs a clear skills profile, learning path, exam evidence, and privacy control.

## Japanese learner preparing IT career

- Wants structured learning, exam practice, and practical job-matching support.
- Needs accessible progress tracking and role-relevant vacancy discovery.

## Company recruiter

- Wants to find candidates who match skills, language level, and work preferences.
- Needs explainable matching, privacy-safe profile access, and application workflows.

## Company engineer reviewer

- Wants technical evidence before interviews.
- Needs reliable summaries of allowed skills, course completion, and exam outcomes.

## Platform admin

- Maintains users, companies, content, exams, vacancies, and policy settings.
- Needs strong access control, audit visibility, and operational oversight.
